Browser web flasher
Tip: tick Erase device when it asks. It is the safest choice, and it fixes most touch and calibration problems after an update.
Needs Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser with Web Serial.
1

Flash the firmware

Click Install and choose your USB port. Tick Erase device. It writes in about 40 seconds.

Format a microSD card as FAT32 and copy everything in the sdcard/ folder to the root of the card.

Power on & calibrate

Tap the markers when asked, and you are in. Tap Wi-Fi to get the QR code for your phone.

The SD card

Everything the wiki reads lives on a plain FAT32 card.

Copy everything inside sdcard/ to the root of the card (cards up to 32 GB format as FAT32 out of the box).

/ (microSD root) ├── config.txt # Wi-Fi name and password ├── wiki/ # categories, catalog, search index, articles, images ├── library/ # field guide + US Army FM 21-76 (62 parts and a PDF) ├── web/ # the phone portal └── swiki-settings.txt # your settings backup, written by the device

Something not working?

  • Touch does nothing or lands in the wrong spot: re-flash with Erase device ticked, then tap the calibration markers. You can redo it later from Settings, or by holding the screen during the splash.
  • Can't connect to the board: use a USB data cable, not a charge-only one. Hold the BOOT button while you click Connect, then let go.
  • No port in the list: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones and Firefox don't support Web Serial.
  • "SD card not found": format the card as FAT32 and copy the sdcard/ contents to the root.
