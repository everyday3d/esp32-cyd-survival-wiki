Complete 3-Step Walkthrough

1

Flash Firmware

Click INSTALL above. When prompted, select your USB port. Select Erase Device if this is a fresh setup. Web Tools will write the flash in ~40 seconds.

2

Load MicroSD Card

Format a microSD card as FAT32. Copy the contents of the sdcard/ folder to the root of the microSD card. Insert the card into the slot on the board.

3

Power On & Explore

Press the RESET button or plug into battery power. Browse 61 illustrated emergency guides & US Army Survival Manual FM 21-76 on-screen, or connect a phone to the SurvivalWiki Wi-Fi network!

⚠️ Troubleshooting & Connection Tips

  • No serial port listed in browser? Make sure you're using a USB-C data cable (many cheap charging cables lack data lines). If on Windows, install the CH340 or CP2102 USB UART driver.
  • Failed to connect or timed out? Hold the BOOT button on the side of the board, click the Install button in your browser, and release BOOT once connecting.
  • "MicroSD card not detected" on screen? Ensure card is formatted FAT32 (not exFAT or NTFS). Insert card all the way until it clicks.
  • Touch screen alignment: Resistive touch panels respond best to a stylus, fingernail, or firm finger press.
