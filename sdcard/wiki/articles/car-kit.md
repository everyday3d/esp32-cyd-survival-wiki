Build a kit once, keep it in the trunk, and check it twice a year.

## All year
- **First aid kit** and any needed medicines. See [Basic Survival Kit](survival-kit.md).
- **Water** (at least a liter or quart per person) and non-perishable snacks.
- **Flashlight** or headlamp with spare batteries, and a whistle.
- **Phone charger** or power bank, and a paper map. See [Map and Compass Basics](map-compass.md).
- **Jumper cables or a jump pack.** See [Jump-Starting a Car](jump-start.md).
- **Spare tire** (check the pressure), jack, lug wrench. See [Changing a Flat Tire](flat-tire.md).
- **Tire plug kit or sealant** and a small air pump that plugs into the car.
- **Window breaker and seat belt cutter.** Keep it within reach. See [Car in Water](car-in-water.md).
- **Warning triangles or reflective flares**, and a high-visibility vest.
- **Small fire extinguisher**, mounted inside the cabin.
- Duct tape, a multi-tool, gloves, rags, zip ties and rope.
- Paper towels and a little cash in small bills.
- Pen and paper, and a list of emergency numbers.

## Add for winter
- **Blankets or a sleeping bag**, warm coat, hat, gloves and boots.
- **Ice scraper and brush**, a small shovel and a bag of sand or cat litter for traction.
- Hand warmers.
- Do not burn candles or stoves inside a closed car. Use the engine for heat and clothes for warmth. See [Stranded in Your Car in Winter](stranded-winter.md).
- **Extra food and water.** A frozen bottle is useless; keep it in the cabin.

## Keep the car ready
- Keep the fuel tank at least half full.
- Check tires, battery, lights, wipers and fluids before long trips and before winter.
- Tell someone your route and when you will arrive. See [Travel and Staying Found](staying-found.md).
