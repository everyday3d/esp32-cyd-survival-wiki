## Choosing a site
- Clear a 3 m (10 ft) circle down to bare soil. Remove leaves and dry grass.
- Stay away from overhanging branches, tree roots, peat and dry grass.
- Do not build against a large rock you want to sit by; wet rocks from rivers can crack or burst when heated.
- Pay attention to wind. Sparks carry far in dry weather.

## Carbon monoxide
> Never burn a fire, stove, charcoal or generator inside a closed tent, car, snow cave or small room. Carbon monoxide has no smell and kills in your sleep.

Signs of poisoning: headache, dizziness, nausea, confusion, sleepiness. Get the person into fresh air at once.

## Putting a fire out
1. Let it burn down to ash if you can.
2. Pour water on it and stir the ashes. Pour more and stir again.
3. If water is scarce, smother with soil or sand and stir, but do not just bury it; buried coals can stay hot for a day.
4. Feel for heat with the back of your hand held close. If it is too hot to touch, it is too hot to leave.

## If a wildfire threatens
- Move to areas with little fuel: rock, gravel, a recently burned area, or across a wide river.
- Move downhill and across the slope, not uphill. Fire races uphill.
- Cover your skin with wool or cotton, not synthetics, and breathe through a damp cloth.
