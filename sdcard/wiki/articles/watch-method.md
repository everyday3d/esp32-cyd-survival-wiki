## Northern Hemisphere
1. Hold the watch flat.
2. Point the **hour hand** at the sun.
3. Halfway between the hour hand and the **12** is **south**.
4. During daylight saving time, use the **1** instead of the 12.

## Southern Hemisphere
1. Point the **12** at the sun.
2. Halfway between the 12 and the hour hand is **north**.
3. During daylight saving time, use the 1 instead of the 12.

## No analog watch?
Draw a clock face on paper or the ground with the hands showing the current time, and use it the same way.

> This method is a rough guide, off by 20 degrees or more in some places and times, and it does not work well near the equator. Check it against other methods.
