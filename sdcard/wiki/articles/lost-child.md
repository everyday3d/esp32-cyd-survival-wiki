## If a child goes missing
1. **Act at once.** Do not wait to see if they come back.
2. **Check water first.** Pools, ponds, buckets, bathtubs, streams and ditches are the biggest danger for young children.
3. **Check hiding places:** closets, cars (including trunks), under beds, sheds, behind furniture.
4. **Call out** and listen. Ask everyone nearby to help.
5. **Call 911 (or your local number) early.** A fast call is better than a late one. Give a **description, clothing, age, where and when last seen, and a photo** if you have it.
6. Leave **one person at the last-seen spot** in case the child returns.
7. In the outdoors, search along **trails, roads and the direction downhill or downwind**, and mark places already checked.
8. Keep calling. Blow a whistle in 3 blasts. Shut off noise sometimes to listen.

## Children who wander
Some children, such as those with autism, tend to wander. Fit a door chime, lock doors high up, tell neighbors, and **search water first** every time.

## Teach kids if they are lost outdoors: "Hug a Tree"
- **Stop and stay put.** Do not wander. A child who stays in one place is found much sooner.
- **Hug a tree**, or sit next to a tree or rock. It is a friend while you wait.
- **Make noise.** Blow a whistle (3 blasts), and shout for help in short bursts.
- **Stay dry and warm.** Put on a jacket. Make a bed of leaves, or cover yourself with a trash bag or leaves.
- **Do not hide** from searchers. They will call your name.
- **Do not go in water,** and do not follow a stream.

Give every child a **whistle** and a bright jacket or hat for hikes. Tell them to stay on the trail and to stop when they cannot see you.

## In a crowd or store
- Stop where you are and call out their name.
- Teach kids to find a person in uniform or a store worker, or a mom with kids, and say "I am lost."
- Ask the staff to lock the exits and call the police.

## When found
Hold them, give water and food, check for injuries and cold or heat illness. See [Hypothermia and Frostbite](hypothermia.md) and [Heat Exhaustion and Heat Stroke](heat-illness.md). **Do not scold.** Praise them for staying put.
