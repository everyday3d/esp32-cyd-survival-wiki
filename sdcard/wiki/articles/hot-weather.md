In heat, shelter means **shade**. The goal is to save water by not sweating.

## Simple shade
- Use natural shade: rock overhangs, the shady side of boulders, bushes.
- Hang a tarp or blanket 30-45 cm (12-18 in) above the ground, with a second layer above it if you have it. The air gap between layers cuts heat a lot.

## Below-ground shelter
1. Dig a trench 45-60 cm (18-24 in) deep, as long as your body, oriented east-west.
2. Pile the sand around the edges.
3. Cover with a tarp, then a second layer with a gap between them.
The ground 30 cm down can be much cooler than the surface. Dig at dawn or dusk, not midday.

## Habits
- Rest in the heat of the day; work and travel at night or early morning.
- Keep your skin covered with loose, light-colored clothing and a hat.
- Do not lie directly on hot ground. Sit on a pack or brush.
- Watch for snakes and scorpions in shade spots; check before you reach in.

> Cars, metal sheds and tents in full sun can become deadly hot. Shade with airflow is better.
