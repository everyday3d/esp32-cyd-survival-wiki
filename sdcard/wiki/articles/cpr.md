Start CPR if a person is **unresponsive and not breathing normally** (or only gasping).

![Hands-only CPR](img/cpr.jpg)

## Adults
1. Call for help. If someone else is there, send them to call and to find an AED (defibrillator).
2. Lay the person on their back on a firm surface.
3. Put the heel of one hand on the **center of the chest** (lower half of the breastbone), the other hand on top.
4. Arms straight, shoulders over your hands. Push **hard and fast**:
- **Depth:** 5-6 cm (2-2.4 in)
- **Rate:** 100-120 per minute (the beat of the song "Stayin' Alive")
- Let the chest come all the way back up between pushes.
5. If trained and willing: after every 30 compressions, give 2 rescue breaths (head tilt, chin lift, pinch nose, breathe until the chest rises).
6. If not trained: keep doing compressions only. That still saves lives.
7. Keep going until the person starts breathing, an AED is ready, help takes over, or you are too exhausted to continue.

## AED
Turn it on and follow the voice instructions. Keep compressions going while it is being set up.

## Children and infants
- **Child:** one or two hands, about 5 cm (2 in) deep.
- **Infant:** two fingers in the center of the chest just below the nipple line, about 4 cm (1.5 in) deep.
- For children and drowning victims, rescue breaths are especially important if you can give them.

> In remote places with no chance of advanced care, CPR rarely restarts the heart after a long time. It is still right to try. Your safety and the group's safety come first.
