## Stay warm with **COLD**
- **C - Clean:** dirty clothes lose insulation.
- **O - avoid Overheating:** remove layers before you sweat.
- **L - Loose layers:** tight clothing cuts circulation.
- **D - Dry:** wet clothing pulls heat away fast. Dry socks matter most.

## Snow trench
1. Dig a trench as long as your body, about 1 m (3 ft) deep, in firm snow.
2. Roof it with branches, skis or a tarp, then pile snow on top.
3. Line the floor with thick insulation: boughs, packs, foam.

## Quinzee (snow mound)
1. Pile snow into a mound about 2 m (7 ft) high. Mix layers as you pile.
2. Let it settle for 1-2 hours.
3. Push sticks about 30 cm (1 ft) into the mound all over, to show wall thickness.
4. Dig a low entrance and hollow it out until you hit the sticks.
5. Poke a fist-sized **air hole** in the roof.

> Always make an air vent in any snow shelter and keep it clear. Keep a digging tool inside with you.

## Tree well
Under a large evergreen, snow is often shallow near the trunk. Dig out the space and roof it with boughs.

## Body heat tips
- Eat before sleeping; digestion makes heat.
- Do light exercise (not to the point of sweating) before getting in your shelter.
- Put a bottle of hot water inside your clothes or bag.
- Share body heat with others.
