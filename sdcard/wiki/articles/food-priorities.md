Food is the last of the big survival needs. Most healthy people can go weeks without it. Hunger is unpleasant, but it is rarely what kills.

## Why food still matters
- It keeps you warm, especially at night in cold weather.
- It keeps your mind sharp and your mood up.
- Long-term survival, past a few days, needs a food plan.

## Rules of thumb
- **Do not spend more energy finding food than it gives you.** Chasing animals is usually a losing trade.
- Eat high-energy foods you carried first, in small regular amounts.
- If water is very short, eat little. Digestion, especially of protein, uses water.
- Easy calories: insects, fish, shellfish from clean water, nuts, and plants you know with certainty.
- Cook all wild meat, fish and shellfish thoroughly.

## Best returns for little effort
1. Fishing with a line, hooks, or simple traps in streams
2. Insects and grubs
3. Familiar plants, nuts and berries you can identify with certainty
4. Trapping small animals (where it is legal and you know how)
