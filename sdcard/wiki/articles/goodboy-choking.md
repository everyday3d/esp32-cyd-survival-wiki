> A choking dog may bite out of panic. Be careful with your hands.

Signs: pawing at the mouth, gagging, blue or pale gums, wheezing, panic.

## Do this now
- Open the mouth and look. If you can see the object and it is easy to reach, hook it out with a finger. Do not push it deeper.
- Small dog: hold it with its head down and give firm pats between the shoulder blades.
- Larger dog: lift the back legs so the head tilts down, then give firm pats between the shoulder blades.
- If that fails: put both hands behind the ribs and give quick, firm thrusts up and forward.
- Go to a vet right away, even if the object comes out.
