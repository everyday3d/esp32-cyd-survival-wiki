Babies depend on you for everything. These basics stay the same when the power is out.

## Feeding
- **Breastfeeding is the safest and easiest** in a disaster. It needs no water, no equipment and no power. Feed on demand and drink plenty yourself.
- **Formula:** ready-to-feed liquid formula is the safest, because it needs no water. Powder is the next choice.
- Mix powder **exactly** as the label says. Never add extra water to stretch it, and never add extra powder. Wrong mixes can harm a baby.
- Use **safe water**: bottled water, or water you have boiled for 1 minute and let cool. See [Making Water Safe to Drink](purifying-water.md).
- Throw away made-up formula that has been out more than 1 hour, or leftover from a feed.
- **No plain water, juice, tea or honey** for babies under 6 months. No honey under 1 year.

## Cleaning bottles
- Wash with soap and safe water and rinse. Then **sanitize** by boiling the bottle parts for 5 minutes, and let them air dry.
- Wash your hands first. Use a clean cup and spoon if you cannot sanitize bottles.

## Warmth
- Babies lose heat fast. Dress them in layers, put a hat on them, and use **skin-to-skin contact** against your chest under your clothes.
- Do not overheat. In hot weather use light clothes and shade, and offer feeds often.
- Do not use open flames, charcoal or camp stoves indoors to keep warm. See [Carbon Monoxide](carbon-monoxide.md).

## Safe sleep
- Lay the baby **on their back, alone, on a firm, flat surface** with no pillows, blankets or soft toys.
- Do not sleep with the baby on a sofa or chair. If you must share a bed, keep soft bedding well away.
- Do not wrap the baby in blankets that can cover the face.

## Diapers and hygiene
- Check diapers often. Change them promptly and use a plastic bag for waste. A baby with **fewer than about 4 wet diapers a day** may be dehydrated.
- Wash hands after every change and before feeding. Use wipes or a cloth and safe water.
- Keep spare diapers, wipes, formula, bottles, medicines and a change of clothes in your go-bag.

## Seek help for
A baby who feeds poorly, is very sleepy or floppy, has a fever, vomiting, diarrhea, blue color or trouble breathing. See [Children Are Different](children-first-aid.md) and [Fever in Children](child-fever.md).
