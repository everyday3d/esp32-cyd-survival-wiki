Short-coated and small dogs lose heat fast. Ears, tail, and paws are first to suffer.

## Signs of trouble
- Shivering, slowing down, whining, lifting paws, refusing to walk.
- Pale or gray skin on ears, tail, or paws can be frostbite.

## Do this
- Get indoors. Wrap in dry blankets. Warm gently with room-temperature (not hot) water and body heat.
- Do not rub frostbitten skin. Do not use a heating pad directly.
- See a vet for frostbite or if the dog is very sleepy.

## Prevention
- Coat or sweater for short-haired dogs. Booties for ice and salt.
- Wipe paws after walks. Road salt and de-icer can poison.
- Never leave a dog in a cold car.
