In an emergency, deal with the biggest threat first. The Rule of 3s is a rough guide to how long a person can last without each basic need.

![The Rule of 3s](img/rule_of_threes.jpg)

## The order
1. **Air** - about 3 minutes. Breathing problems, choking, drowning, smoke and severe bleeding come first.
2. **Shelter** - about 3 hours in harsh weather. Cold, wet and wind kill faster than thirst. Stay dry and insulated.
3. **Water** - about 3 days. Less in heat or with hard work.
4. **Food** - about 3 weeks. Food matters for morale and energy, but it is the last priority.

> These are averages, not promises. Cold water, heat, injury, age and illness shorten every limit.

## Using it
- Before you build anything, check for immediate danger: fire, rising water, traffic, falling rocks.
- Treat life-threatening injuries next.
- Then protect yourself from the weather, then find water.
- Spend energy on food only once the first three are handled.
