Heavy bleeding can kill in minutes. Act fast.

## 1. Direct pressure
- Put a cloth or gauze on the wound (bare hands if nothing else) and **press hard** with both hands.
- Hold pressure for at least **10 minutes** without lifting to check.
- If blood soaks through, add more material on top and keep pressing.

## 2. Pack deep wounds
For a deep wound in the thigh, armpit, groin or neck, push gauze or clean cloth **into** the wound, filling it, then press hard on top for at least 3 minutes (10 if plain cloth).

## 3. Tourniquet for arms and legs
Use a tourniquet when bleeding from an arm or leg is life-threatening and pressure does not stop it, or you cannot hold pressure (for example, several injured people).

![Tourniquet placement](img/tourniquet.jpg)

1. Place it **5-7 cm (2-3 in) above the wound**, never on a joint. If unsure, place it high on the limb.
2. Tighten until the bleeding **stops**. It will hurt a lot. That is expected.
3. **Write the time** on the tourniquet or the person's skin.
4. Do not loosen it. Leave it for medical staff.
5. If bleeding continues, add a second tourniquet just above the first.

## Improvised tourniquet
A strip of cloth at least 4 cm (1.5 in) wide, tied around the limb, with a stick through the knot. Twist the stick until bleeding stops, then tie the stick in place. Thin cord or wire cuts the skin and does not work well.

> After bleeding is controlled, keep the person warm and lying down. Watch for [Shock](shock.md).
