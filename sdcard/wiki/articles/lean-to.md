A lean-to is quick to build and pairs well with a fire in front. It blocks wind and rain from one side.

![Lean-to with fire reflector](img/lean_to.jpg)

## Building it
1. Find two trees about 2-3 m apart, or drive two forked sticks into the ground.
2. Tie or wedge a strong **ridge pole** across them at about chest height.
3. Lean long poles against the ridge pole on the windward side, close together, at about 45-60 degrees.
4. Weave smaller branches across the poles.
5. Cover with layers of bark, leafy branches, grass or a tarp, starting at the bottom so each layer overlaps the one below, like shingles.
6. Make a thick bed of dry insulation inside.

## Adding a fire reflector
Build a fire about one step in front of the open side. Behind the fire, stack green logs between stakes to make a wall. It throws heat back into the shelter.

> The back of the lean-to must face the wind. Check the wind direction before you start, and watch for changes.
