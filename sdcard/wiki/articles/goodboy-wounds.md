> Never leave a tight band on. Check often that the paw or limb is not cold or swollen.

## Bleeding
- Press a clean cloth or gauze firmly on the wound. Keep pressing. Do not peek.
- Add more cloth over the top if it soaks through.
- Wrap snugly with self-sticking wrap (not tight). Leave the toes showing so you can check them.
- Get to a vet for deep cuts, anything that will not stop in a few minutes, or any puncture.

## Paws
- Rinse dirt out with clean water.
- Cover with a clean sock or bootie to keep it clean on the way to the vet.

## Stop the licking
- Licking reopens wounds. Use a cone or a clean sock until a vet looks at it.
