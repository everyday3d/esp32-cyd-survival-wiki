> Only for an unresponsive dog that is not breathing. Call a vet on the way. Someone else should drive if possible.

## Steps
- Lay the dog on its right side on a firm surface.
- Chest compressions: hands over the widest part of the chest, push about one third to one half of the chest depth, 100 to 120 per minute.
- Small dogs and puppies: use one hand or two fingers around the chest.
- Rescue breaths: close the mouth, breathe into the nose until the chest rises. Two breaths after every 30 compressions.
- Keep going until the dog responds or you reach a vet.

Learn this in a certified pet first aid class. This card is a reminder, not training.
