Only change a tire on **flat, firm ground** well away from traffic. If you cannot, call for help.

## You need
Spare tire (check its pressure), jack, lug wrench, and the car manual. A flashlight, gloves and a piece of wood to put under the jack on soft ground all help.

## Steps
1. Park on level ground, put it in Park, set the parking brake and turn on the hazard lights. Everyone gets out of the car and away from traffic.
2. Put a rock or block against the wheel **opposite** the flat so the car cannot roll.
3. Remove the hubcap. **Loosen the lug nuts a half turn with the wrench while the wheel is still on the ground.** Do not remove them yet.
4. Find the jack points in the manual. Place the jack on solid ground at the right point. Raise the car until the flat is about 5 cm (2 in) off the ground.
5. Remove the nuts and pull the wheel straight off.
6. Mount the spare on the studs. Put the nuts on by hand with the tapered sides facing in.
7. Tighten the nuts snugly in a **star pattern** (opposite nuts, not around in a circle).
8. Lower the car, remove the jack, then tighten all the nuts firmly as hard as you can, again in a star pattern.
9. Put the flat and tools away.

## Important
- **Never put any part of your body under a car that is held up only by a jack.**
- Never start or run the engine with the car on the jack.
- A small temporary spare ("donut") is only for short trips. Stay below about 80 km/h (50 mph) and get the proper tire fixed soon.
- Check the spare's pressure and re-check the lug nuts after about 50 km (30 miles).
- If a tire blows out while driving, hold the wheel firmly, ease off the gas and brake gently. Do not stomp on the brake.
- A can of tire sealant or a plug kit can get you to a shop for a small puncture in the tread (not the sidewall).
