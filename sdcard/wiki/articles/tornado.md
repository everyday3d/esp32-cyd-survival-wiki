A tornado can form with little warning. Know where you will go **before** the sky turns.

## Watch and warning
- **Watch:** conditions are right for tornadoes. Stay alert and know your shelter.
- **Warning:** a tornado is happening or about to. **Take shelter now.**

## Signs
- Dark, greenish sky and large hail
- A rotating, low cloud or a funnel
- A loud, steady roar like a freight train
- A cloud of debris near the ground, even if you see no funnel

## Where to go
- **Best:** a basement or storm cellar. Get under sturdy furniture or a workbench.
- **No basement:** the lowest floor, in a small interior room with no windows (bathroom, closet, hallway). Put as many walls as you can between you and the outside.
- **Cover up:** use a mattress, blankets or a bike helmet. Protect your head and neck with your arms.
- **Mobile homes and vehicles are not safe.** Get to a sturdy building before the storm arrives.

## If you are caught outside
- Do not try to outrun a tornado in a car in traffic or a city.
- If there is no building, lie flat in the lowest ground you can find (a ditch), face down, and cover your head and neck with your hands.
- **Do not shelter under a highway overpass.** Wind speeds can be higher there.

## Afterward
- Expect more storms. Stay in shelter until the warning ends.
- Watch for downed power lines, broken glass, nails and gas leaks. See [Downed Power Lines](downed-power-lines.md) and [Gas Leak](gas-leak.md).
- Check for injuries and give first aid. See [First Aid Priorities](first-aid-priorities.md).
- Text instead of calling so lines stay open for emergencies.
