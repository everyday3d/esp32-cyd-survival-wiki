> Radiation from fallout fades fast. Follow official instructions on when to leave. If none are available, use these rules.

## The 7-10 rule of thumb
- Every time the time since the blast **multiplies by 7**, the radiation falls to **one tenth**.
- After 7 hours it is about 1/10 of what it was at 1 hour.
- After 49 hours (about 2 days) it is about 1/100.
- After about 2 weeks it is about 1/1000.
- It is a rough guide for fallout only. It does not replace official advice.

## How long to stay
- At least 24 hours, and longer if you can. The first day is the most dangerous.
- If you must go out for essentials, keep it short. Cover your skin, nose and mouth, then follow the cleaning steps when you return.
- Move only if officials tell you to, or if the building is damaged or on fire.

## Potassium iodide (KI)
- KI protects the thyroid from radioactive iodine. It does **not** protect from other radiation.
- Take it only if public health officials tell you to. Too much can hurt you.
- It does not replace sheltering.

## Helping others
- Check on neighbours who cannot move easily, if you can do it without going outside.
- Share information from official sources, not rumours.
