Fire can fill a house with deadly smoke in two or three minutes. **Get out first and call for help from outside.**

## If the alarm sounds or you see fire
1. **Get out.** Do not stop for belongings, phones or pets.
2. **Crawl low** under smoke, where the air is cleaner.
3. Before opening a door, **feel it with the back of your hand.** If it is hot, do not open it. Use another way out.
4. Close doors behind you to slow the fire.
5. Go to your family's meeting place. Count everyone.
6. **Call 911 from outside.** Never go back in. Tell firefighters if anyone is missing.

## If you are trapped
- Close the door and stuff towels or clothes (wet if possible) in the cracks. Cover vents.
- Go to a window. Open it a little, or break it if you have to, and call or wave a bright cloth.
- Call 911 and say exactly which room you are in.
- Stay low near the window.
- If your clothes catch fire, **stop, drop and roll.** See [Burns](burns.md).

## Smoke alarms and plans
- Put an alarm on every level and outside every sleeping area. Test monthly and replace batteries yearly. Replace alarms every 10 years.
- Make a home escape plan with **two ways out of every room**. Practice it twice a year, including in the dark.
- Fit a ladder in upper bedrooms if you have no other escape.

## Kitchen fires
- **Grease fire:** turn off the heat and slide a lid over the pan. **Never use water.** Baking soda or salt can smother a small one.
- Oven fire: keep the door closed and turn the oven off.
- Never carry a burning pan outside.

## Small fires and extinguishers
Use one only if the fire is small, you have a clear exit behind you and the room is not filling with smoke. Remember **PASS**: Pull the pin, Aim at the base, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. If in doubt, **get out.** See [Fire Safety](fire-safety.md).
