## How much
At least **3 days** of food for everyone in the home. **Two weeks** is better. Include pets.

## What to store
Choose food your family already eats, that needs little or no cooking and little water:
- Canned meat, fish, beans, soups, vegetables and fruit
- Peanut butter, nuts, trail mix
- Crackers, granola and energy bars
- Dried fruit
- Ready-to-eat cereal, instant oats
- Shelf-stable milk or powdered milk
- Rice, pasta and dried beans (only if you can cook with water to spare)
- Comfort food: chocolate, coffee, tea, sweets. Morale matters.
- Baby food and formula if you have an infant; special foods for anyone with allergies or medical needs

## Do not forget
- A **manual can opener**
- Plates, cups and utensils you can use without washing
- A way to cook without power: camp stove and fuel, used **outdoors only**

## Keeping it fresh
- Store in a cool, dry, dark place, off the floor.
- **First in, first out:** use the oldest food first and put new purchases at the back.
- Check dates twice a year (for example, when the clocks change).
- Throw out cans that are bulging, leaking, rusted deeply or dented along a seam.

> Salty foods make you thirsty. If water is short, choose low-salt foods and canned foods packed in water or juice.
