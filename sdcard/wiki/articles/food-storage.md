## Keep animals away
- Cook and eat at least 60 m (200 ft) downwind from where you sleep.
- Hang food, trash and anything with a smell (toothpaste, soap) from a branch at least 4 m (12 ft) up and 1.5 m (5 ft) out from the trunk, or use a bear canister.
- Never keep food in your shelter in bear country.

## Avoid food poisoning
- Wash hands with soap, or use sanitizer, before handling food.
- Boil water used for cooking and washing dishes.
- Eat cooked food right away. In warm weather, cooked food left out for more than 2 hours can make you sick.
- Throw away cans that bulge, leak, spurt when opened, or smell bad.

## Preserving meat and fish
- **Drying:** cut into thin strips, no thicker than 6 mm (1/4 in), and dry in sun and wind or over a low, smoky fire until brittle.
- **Smoking:** keep strips in heavy smoke over a low fire for many hours. Smoke keeps insects off and helps preserve.
- **Cold:** in winter, freeze meat outside in a shaded, animal-proof place.

> Thin strips and complete drying are key. Thick or damp meat spoils quickly.
