This works anywhere in the world on a sunny day.

![Shadow-stick method](img/shadow_stick.jpg)

## Steps
1. Push a straight stick, about 1 m (3 ft) long, upright into flat ground.
2. Mark the tip of its shadow with a small stone. This is your **west** mark.
3. Wait **15 minutes** or more. The shadow tip moves.
4. Mark the new shadow tip. This is your **east** mark.
5. Draw a line from the first mark to the second: that line runs **west to east**.
6. Stand with the first mark on your left and the second on your right. You are now facing **north**.

## Tips
- The longer you wait between marks, the more accurate the result.
- It is most accurate around midday and near the equinoxes, and less accurate early and late in the day.
- At local noon, the shadow is at its shortest and points roughly north (in the Northern Hemisphere) or south (in the Southern Hemisphere), away from the sun.
