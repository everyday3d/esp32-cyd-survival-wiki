Standing near moving traffic is one of the most dangerous places to be. Think about traffic first.

## If the car is slowing down or dying
- Turn on the hazard lights right away.
- Steer toward the **right shoulder** or an exit, a parking lot or any wide, flat spot well off the road. Coast as far as you can.
- Do not slam on the brakes. Brake gently.

## Once you have stopped
- Turn the wheels away from the road. Set the parking brake and put the car in Park.
- Keep the hazard lights on. Raise the hood or tie a white cloth to the door handle or antenna.
- **Call for help.** Say where you are: road name, direction, mile marker or the last exit.
- If you are on a busy highway with no barrier and cannot reach safety, **stay in the car with your seat belt on** and call for help. Do not stand behind or beside the car.
- If you can get to safety, **get out on the side away from traffic** and move well away from the road, behind a guardrail or up a bank.
- Do not try to repair the car with traffic passing close by.

## Safety
- Be careful with strangers. Stay in your locked car if someone stops, speak through a cracked window and ask them to call for help. Do not accept a ride if you do not have to.
- At night, turn on the dome light so you can be seen and avoid sitting in the dark.
- If you are not sure where you are, keep your phone charged. See [Radio and Phone Tips](emergency-radio.md).

## Simple things to check
- **Out of fuel:** you need a can of fuel or a tow.
- **Won't start, clicks:** probably a weak battery. See [Jump-Starting a Car](jump-start.md).
- **Overheating:** pull over, turn off the engine and **do not open the radiator cap** while it is hot. Wait at least 30 minutes.
- **Flat tire:** see [Changing a Flat Tire](flat-tire.md).
- **Smoke from the hood:** see [Vehicle Fire](car-fire.md).
