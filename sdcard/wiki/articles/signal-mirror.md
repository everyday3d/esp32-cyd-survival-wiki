A mirror flash can be seen up to 15 km (10 miles) or more on a sunny day. It is one of the best rescue signals.

![Aiming a signal mirror](img/signal_mirror.jpg)

## Aiming without a sighting hole
1. Hold the mirror just under your eye, shiny side out.
2. Stretch out your other arm and make a **V** with two fingers. Frame the target (aircraft, boat, hilltop) inside the V.
3. Tilt the mirror until the spot of sunlight lands on your fingers.
4. Keep the light in the V and rock the mirror slightly so it sweeps across the target.

## Mirrors with a sighting hole
Look through the hole at the target. You will see a bright aiming spot; tilt the mirror until the spot sits on the target.

## Tips
- Anything shiny works: a phone screen, CD, polished can lid, foil.
- Sweep the horizon often, even when you see nothing. Pilots may see you before you see them.
- Do not keep flashing a pilot who has clearly seen you; it can blind them. Switch to ground signals.
