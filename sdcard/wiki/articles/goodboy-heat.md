> Heatstroke can kill within minutes. Cool first, then drive to a vet right after.

Signs: heavy panting, thick drool, bright red gums, wobbling, vomiting, collapse.

## Do this now
- Move the dog into shade or an air-conditioned space.
- Wet the dog with cool (not ice-cold) water, especially the belly, armpits, and paws.
- Fan the dog. Offer small sips of water. Do not force it.
- Stop cooling once the dog is shivering or breathing settles. Overcooling is a risk too.
- Go to a vet even if the dog seems better. Organ damage can show up later.

## Prevention
- Never leave a dog in a parked car. Cracked windows do not help.
- Walk in the cool of the day. Pavement burns paws long before it feels hot to you.
- Flat-faced and heavy-coated dogs overheat fastest.
