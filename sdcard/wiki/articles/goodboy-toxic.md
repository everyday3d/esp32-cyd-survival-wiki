> Call a vet or a pet poison line immediately. Do not wait for symptoms. Do not induce vomiting unless a vet tells you to.

## Dangerous to dogs
- Xylitol (sugar-free gum, candy, some peanut butters). Very dangerous, even in small amounts.
- Grapes, raisins, and currants.
- Chocolate, especially dark chocolate and baking cocoa.
- Onions, garlic, chives, leeks.
- Macadamia nuts, alcohol, raw bread dough, caffeine.
- Antifreeze (sweet-tasting) and rat poison.
- Human medicines, especially ibuprofen, naproxen, and acetaminophen.

## What to tell them
- Your dog's weight, what was eaten, how much, and when.
- Keep the package or label with you.

Poison lines (a fee may apply): ASPCA Animal Poison Control 888-426-4435. Pet Poison Helpline 855-764-7661.
