## Before you go
Tell someone your route and when to expect you. Carry a map of the area.

## Marking your trail
- Break branches at eye level, pointing your direction.
- Stack small rock piles (cairns) or make arrows with sticks.
- Leave notes at junctions: time, date, direction and condition.

## If you are lost and decide to move
- Pick one direction and stick to it. People who wander in circles get more lost.
- Go downhill and follow water: streams lead to rivers, and rivers to roads and towns. But avoid steep canyons and waterfalls.
- Climb to a high point (safely) to look for roads, buildings, smoke or power lines.
- Follow power lines, fences, pipelines or railways. They lead somewhere.
- Travel in daylight. Stop and make camp well before dark.

> In fog, darkness or a whiteout, stop and wait. Walking blind leads to falls and getting more lost.

## Signs of people
Roads, trails, fences, cut stumps, tire tracks, livestock, and the sound of traffic, dogs or machines.
