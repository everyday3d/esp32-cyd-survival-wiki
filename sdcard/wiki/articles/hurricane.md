**Storm surge** (the wall of seawater pushed ashore) is the deadliest part of a hurricane. Wind is second.

## Before the storm
- Know if you live in an evacuation zone. **If officials say evacuate, go.** Leave early to beat traffic and flooded roads.
- Fill the car with fuel and withdraw some cash. Take medicines, documents, phone chargers, pets and a go-bag.
- Fill the bathtub and containers with water. See [Storing Water at Home](water-storage.md).
- Bring in or tie down anything the wind can pick up. Board up or shutter windows if you can.
- Freeze water bottles to keep the fridge cold longer.

## If you stay (outside surge zones only)
- Pick a small interior room on the lowest level that will not flood. A closet or bathroom with no windows is best.
- Stay away from windows and glass doors. Cover yourself with mattresses or blankets.
- Have a radio, flashlight, first aid kit and water with you.
- **The calm eye is not the end.** Winds return from the other direction, often fiercely. Stay inside until officials say it is over.
- If water rises in your home, go to the highest floor. Do not retreat into a closed attic.

## After the storm
- Stay out of floodwater. See [Floods and Flash Floods](flood.md).
- Do not drive through standing water or over damaged roads and bridges.
- Treat every downed line as live. See [Downed Power Lines](downed-power-lines.md).
- **Never run a generator, grill or camp stove indoors.** Carbon monoxide poisoning kills many people after storms. See [Generator Safety](generator-safety.md).
- Check on neighbors, especially older people. Document damage with photos before cleanup.
