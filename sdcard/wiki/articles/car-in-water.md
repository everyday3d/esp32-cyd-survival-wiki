A car in deep water usually floats for 30 to 60 seconds before it sinks. **Act at once.** Do not call for help first and do not wait for the car to fill with water.

## Remember: Seat belt, Window, Children, Out
1. **Seat belt:** unbuckle yourself.
2. **Window:** open or break a **side window** now. Electric windows often still work for the first moments. If not, use a **window-breaker tool** (hit it at a corner of the glass, not the center). Windshields are hard to break.
3. **Children:** unbuckle the oldest children first so they can help. Get the youngest out next. Hold on to them.
4. **Out:** climb out of the window, head first or feet first, and swim away from the car to safety.

## Important
- **The door will not open** until the water pressure inside and outside is nearly equal. Do not waste time on it.
- If you cannot get a window open, stay calm and move to the highest air pocket. When the car is nearly full of water the pressure equalizes and the door can open. Take a breath, open the door and swim out.
- Keep calm. Focus on one step at a time.

## If you are caught in floodwater
Leave the vehicle before the water gets high. See [Floods and Flash Floods](flood.md).

## Prevent it
- **Never drive into water over a road.** Turn around.
- Keep a window-breaker and seat belt cutter within reach, such as on the sun visor or key ring. Not in the glove box.
- Learn the steps with your family.

After you are out, get warm and dry and watch for hypothermia. See [Hypothermia and Frostbite](hypothermia.md).
