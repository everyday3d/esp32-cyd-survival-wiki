## Pack for your dog
- Water and a collapsible bowl.
- At least a week of food in a sealed container.
- Medications and a note of the dose and schedule.
- Leash, spare leash, and a harness that fits.
- Vaccination records, microchip number, and a recent photo of your dog.
- Waste bags, towel, and an old blanket or bed.
- Booties, a muzzle (a hurt dog can bite), and a first-aid kit.
- Collar with ID tags that have your cell phone number.
- A crate or carrier that fits your dog.

Check expiry dates twice a year. Keep the bag by the door with the human go-bag.
