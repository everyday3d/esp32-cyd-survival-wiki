## During a seizure
1. **Stay calm and time it.**
2. Move hard or sharp objects away. Put something soft under their head.
3. Loosen anything tight around the neck.
4. **Do not hold them down** and **do not put anything in their mouth**. They cannot swallow their tongue.
5. Stay with them until it ends.

## After it stops
- Roll them onto their **side** in the recovery position to keep the airway clear.
- Check their breathing.
- They will likely be confused and very tired. Speak calmly, tell them what happened, and let them rest.
- Do not give food or drink until they are fully awake.

## Get emergency help if
- The seizure lasts **more than 5 minutes**
- Another seizure starts soon after the first
- They do not wake up, or have trouble breathing afterward
- It is their first seizure, or you do not know their history
- It happened in water
- They are injured, pregnant, or have diabetes
- They have a high fever or recent head injury

> Seizures can also be caused by heat stroke, very low blood sugar, head injury or poisoning. Treat the cause if you can: cool someone overheated, and give sugar to a diabetic once they are awake and can swallow.
