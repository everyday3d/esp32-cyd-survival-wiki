The debris hut keeps you warm with no fire, sleeping bag or tools. It works like a giant sleeping bag made of leaves.

![Debris hut cross-section](img/debris_hut.jpg)

## Building it
1. Find a **ridge pole** a bit longer than your height.
2. Prop one end on a stump, rock or a pair of crossed sticks about waist high. The other end rests on the ground.
3. Lie down under it to check: it should be just big enough to fit you. Bigger means colder.
4. Lean sticks along both sides of the pole like ribs, close together.
5. Lay a lattice of small branches over the ribs so leaves do not fall through.
6. Pile on dry leaves, grass, pine needles and debris until the walls are **60-90 cm (2-3 ft) thick**. Thicker for colder nights.
7. Add some branches on top to keep the wind from blowing the leaves off.
8. Stuff the inside with dry leaves for a thick bed, and keep a pile by the entrance.
9. Crawl in feet first and pull the leaf pile in to plug the door.

> It takes a lot of debris, often a few hours of work. Start early and gather more than you think you need.
