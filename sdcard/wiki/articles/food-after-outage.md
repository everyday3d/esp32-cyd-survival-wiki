## Keep it closed
- **Do not open the fridge or freezer** more than you must.
- A closed fridge stays cold for about **4 hours**.
- A full freezer holds for about **48 hours**, a half-full one for about 24 hours.
- Group food together and fill empty space with ice, frozen jugs or bags. Use a cooler with ice for the fridge items.

## The 2-hour rule
- Perishable food that has been above **4 C (40 F)** for more than **2 hours** (1 hour if above 32 C / 90 F) should be thrown away.
- A thermometer in the fridge tells you the real temperature.

## Throw away (fridge)
- Meat, poultry, fish and eggs
- Milk, soft cheese, yogurt, cream
- Cooked leftovers, deli food and cut fruit or vegetables
- Anything that smells wrong

## Usually safe
- Hard cheese, butter, margarine
- Whole fruits and vegetables, peanut butter, jelly, ketchup, mustard
- Unopened bottled drinks

## Freezer
- If food still has ice crystals or is cold to the touch (at or below 4 C / 40 F), it is usually safe to refreeze or cook.
- If it has thawed completely and was warm for over 2 hours, throw away meat, poultry, fish and dairy.
- Don't refreeze ice cream or other items that have melted.

## Cooking and eating
- Cook thawed food promptly. Cook meat to a safe temperature (see the cooking guide).
- **When in doubt, throw it out.** You cannot tell by smell or taste.
- Do not taste food to check.

> Use up the freezer first. Eat fresh and refrigerated items early, then canned and dry goods.
