> Fallout is dust. Taking it off your body and keeping it out of your food and water removes most of the danger.

## If you were outside during fallout
1. **Take off your outer clothing** before you go in, or right away once inside. Do it gently so the dust doesn't blow around. This removes most of the contamination.
2. Put the clothes in a sealed plastic bag and leave it away from people and pets.
3. **Wash**: shower or wash with soap, water and a gentle scrub. Wash your hair with shampoo.
4. Do not use conditioner, because it can bind dust to your hair.
5. Do not scrub hard or scratch your skin. Blow your nose, wipe your eyelids, and wipe your ears.
6. Put on clean clothes.
7. If you cannot shower, wipe your skin and hair with a clean damp cloth or wipes.

## Pets
- Wash them with soap and water, and keep them inside.

## Food and water
- Food in sealed containers, like cans and bottles, is safe once you wipe the outside.
- Food in the fridge or freezer, or in closed packages, is safe.
- Do not eat food that was outside and uncovered, or that may have dust on it.
- Water in pipes, water heaters, and sealed bottles is safe to drink. Water that was open to the air outside may not be.
- Do not drink from open sources until officials say it is safe.

## Hurt or sick
- Treat injuries as normal first aid. Being contaminated does not make a hurt person a danger to you. Clean the wound if you can.
- Get urgent medical care when it is safe to go out.
