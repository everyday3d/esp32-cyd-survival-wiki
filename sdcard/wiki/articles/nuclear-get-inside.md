> The three steps are the same for every nuclear or radiation emergency: **Get inside. Stay inside. Stay tuned.** Do not try to outrun fallout.

## If you see a flash
- Do not look at it. Turn away and close your eyes.
- Take cover right away behind anything solid, or drop flat and cover your head. The blast wave arrives seconds later.
- Stay down for at least 30 seconds, then get inside.

## 1. Get inside
- Go into the nearest solid building. Brick or concrete is best.
- The best spots are the middle of a basement or the middle of a large building, away from outside walls and the roof.
- More mass between you and the outdoors is better. Fallout is dust, and dust loses strength with distance and time.
- If you are in a car, get into a building. A car does not stop radiation.

## 2. Stay inside
- Stay for at least 24 hours, unless officials tell you otherwise.
- Close and lock the doors and windows. Turn off fans, air conditioning and heating that pull in outside air. Close the fireplace damper.
- Move to the lowest level, in the middle of the building.
- Fallout is strongest in the first hours and drops quickly.

## 3. Stay tuned
- Use a battery or hand-crank radio, a phone, or this device's Wi-Fi if someone has loaded updates.
- Listen for where to go and how long to stay. Officials may tell you to move to a better shelter.
- Do not use the phone for calls unless it is an emergency, so the network stays open.

## If you were outside
- Take off your outer clothes before you go in. This removes most of the dust. See "Fallout: Cleaning Up and Staying Safe".
- Wash your skin and hair with soap and water.
