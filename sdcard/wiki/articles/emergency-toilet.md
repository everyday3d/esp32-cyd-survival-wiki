## At home when the water is off
**Bucket toilet:**
1. Use a 5-gallon (20 L) bucket with a snug lid, or line a toilet bowl with a heavy trash bag.
2. Add a little **kitty litter, sawdust, ash or dry soil** after each use to cut smell and flies.
3. Tie the bag shut when it is half full. Double-bag it and store it in a lidded container outdoors away from the house, well and animals.
4. Wash your hands afterward. See the handwashing guide.

**Do not** flush if sewers may be backed up or the water is off. Do not pour waste near wells, streams or garden food.

## In the field
**Cathole (short stays, small groups):**
- Dig a hole about 15-20 cm (6-8 in) deep, **at least 60 m (200 ft)** from water, trails and camp.
- Cover it fully with soil when done.
- Carry used paper out in a bag, or burn it completely. Never leave it on top.

**Trench latrine (a group staying several days):**
- Dig a trench about 30 cm (1 ft) wide and 60-90 cm (2-3 ft) deep, downhill and downwind of camp and water. Cover each use with soil. Fill it in when you leave.

## Keep people healthy
- Wash hands after every trip and before handling food.
- Keep flies off waste and food. Cover the toilet.
- Dispose of diapers, bandages and wipes in sealed bags, not in the trench.
- Sick people with diarrhea need their own bucket. Clean it with a bleach solution (1 part bleach to 9 parts water).

> Poor sanitation causes more sickness than most dangers on this list. Treat it as a first-day priority.
