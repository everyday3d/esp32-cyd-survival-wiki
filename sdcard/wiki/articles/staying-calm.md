Survival is mostly mental. People with very little gear have survived because they kept their heads. People with plenty have died because they panicked.

## Calm yourself in a minute
**Box breathing:** breathe in for 4 seconds, hold for 4, breathe out for 4, hold for 4. Repeat four or five times. It slows your heart and helps you think.

Then use [STOP](stop.md): Stop, Think, Observe, Plan.

## Keep going
- **Give yourself small goals.** "Get the shelter roof done before dark" is easier than "survive."
- **Keep a routine:** set times to check signals, collect water and firewood, eat and sleep.
- **Stay busy.** Useful work keeps fear down.
- **Look after your body.** Cold, thirst, hunger and lack of sleep all make your thinking worse. Fix them early.
- **Keep a log.** Write the date, what you did, and what you plan. It helps you think, and helps rescuers later.

## In a group
- Choose one leader to make final decisions, and listen to everyone first.
- Give every person a job, even children and injured people. Feeling useful matters.
- Check on each other. Watch for anyone who goes quiet, stops eating or wanders off.
- Keep the group together. Never let anyone leave camp alone without saying where and when they will be back.

## With children
- Stay calm in front of them. They take their cues from you.
- Explain simply and honestly what is happening and what the plan is.
- Give them simple tasks and praise.
- Teach them: **if you are lost, stay put, and hug a tree.** Make noise, answer anyone calling your name, and do not hide from searchers.

> Rescue usually comes. Most people who are reported missing are found within a day or two.
