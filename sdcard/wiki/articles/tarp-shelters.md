A tarp is the fastest shelter there is. Even a large trash bag or emergency blanket can become a roof.

![Tarp A-frame](img/tarp_shelter.jpg)

## A-frame
1. Tie a cord tight between two trees at waist to chest height.
2. Hang the tarp over the cord so both sides are equal.
3. Stake or weight the corners down. Pull them out until the tarp is tight.
4. In wind, set the windward side lower or right to the ground.

## Lean-to tarp
Tie the top edge of the tarp along the ridgeline and stake the bottom edge to the ground on the windward side. Good with a fire in front.

## Tips
- No grommets? Wrap a small stone in the tarp corner and tie the cord around it.
- Angle the roof steeply so rain runs off.
- Dig a shallow trench uphill of the shelter to steer water around it.
- Tie cord with a knot you can untie: a taut-line hitch or truckers hitch lets you tighten the line.

> An emergency blanket reflects heat back to you, but it holds condensation. Wrap it outside your insulation, not against your skin, when you can.
